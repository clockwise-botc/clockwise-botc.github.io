Clockwise

Privacy policy

Clockwise is a browser extension and dashboard that keeps statistics for a group of people who play Blood on the Clocktower together on botc.app. It isn't made by or connected with botc.app or The Pandemonium Institute.

What the extension records

Only on botc.app, and only Blood on the Clocktower games you are in (playing, storytelling or watching) while the extension is installed. For each game it records what botc.app shows everyone in the room at the end of the game:

  • the room name, the script, when the game started and ended, and who won;
  • each seat's botc.app username and account ID, character, alignment, and whether and when they died;
  • nominations, votes, dead votes used, the storyteller's reminder tokens and demon bluffs;
  • the storyteller's username, and the username of the person whose extension recorded the game.

You can tell it not to record a game (in the extension's menu, “Don't record”); that game is kept only in your browser and isn't shared.

Apart from botc.app, the extension only runs on the Clockwise website itself, to share your sign-in between the two. It does not read any other website, your browsing history, chat, video, audio, or anything you type.

Your account

To use Clockwise you sign in once, with a username and password you choose (no email). Creating an account needs the community password. Your password is stored scrambled (salted and hashed), so nobody can read it, including the organiser. Your account can also hold:

  • which player in the games is you (Clockwise fills this in from the botc.app account you're logged in to, and you can change or remove it);
  • whether your stats are private, and the communities you've made from the rooms you play in.

On a computer with the extension, your sign-in is kept in the extension and on the Clockwise website, so you don't sign in twice. Signing out of either signs you out of both.

Where it goes

Games are kept in your browser and uploaded to one Google Sheet run by the community's organiser, through a Google Apps Script. If several people record the same game, it is stored once. The extension also downloads botc.app's public character images and fonts so the dashboard can draw the game's tokens.

Who can see it

The stats are shown on the community dashboard, which needs an account in the community. Members can see the community's games (those filed under Escape or Rhap), including other players' usernames and statistics. A game filed only under someone's own community is shown just to the people who recorded it. If you make your stats private, only you can see your player page; your games still count in everyone's stats. A small number of people with an editor key can correct or delete games, see account usernames and claimed players, and set a new password for someone who has forgotten theirs. The Google Sheet itself is private to the organiser.

Clockwise has no adverts or analytics, and the data is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Removing your data

Ask the community's organiser (the person who shared Clockwise with you) to remove games, delete your account, or stop recording under your name. Uninstalling the extension deletes the copy kept in your browser and stops it recording.
